### 1. Evaluation Task
Assign an integer score from 0 to 4 to each of the 8 dimensions below, providing evidence fields (items length must be exactly 8).

Scoring Logic (Positive Coding):
- High scores (3-4): Superior client state (open, cooperative, deeply engaged, demonstrating insight and change)
- Low scores (0-1): Poor client state (marked resistance, avoiding present focus, lack of exploration)

### 2. Unified Scoring Criteria (0-4 Integer; Default = 2)
General Scale:
0: Strong contradictory evidence (pronounced opposite state / severe issue)
1: Contradictory evidence or marked deficit
2: Insufficient evidence / ambiguous / average (default score)
3: Clear positive evidence (at least 1), with zero contradictions
4: Strong positive evidence (at least 2 independent occurrences), with zero contradictions (rare)

Dimension Definitions (Must strictly use the following exact item names):
1) 1. Resistance (Reverse: Openness): Whether client is open and cooperative (higher score = lower resistance)
2) 2. Agreement (Endorsement): Whether client genuinely understands and endorses counselor remarks (not mere polite nodding)
3) 3. Appropriate Request (Willingness to seek help): Whether client actively and appropriately seeks clarification/advice (not helpless dependence)
4) 4. Narrative (Reverse: Present focus): Whether client is caught in storytelling or avoiding the present (higher score = more focused on present)
5) 5. Cognitive Exploration (Depth): Whether client actively explores underlying beliefs, thoughts, and behavioral patterns
6) 6. Emotional Exploration (Depth): Whether client contacts and verbalizes immediate emotional experiences
7) 7. Insight (Awareness): Whether new understanding, pattern recognition, or taking personal responsibility emerges
8) 8. Therapeutic Change (Actionability): Whether signs of change or concrete action plans emerge

### 3. Scoring Rules (Evidence first, then score)
For each dimension, extract evidence:
- evidence_pos: Verbatim snippets showing high-quality engagement for this dimension (maximum 2; empty [] if none)
- evidence_neg: Verbatim snippets showing low-quality / opposite state (maximum 2; empty [] if none)

Score Gating (Mandatory):
- Default score = 2: Unmentioned / insufficient evidence / unclear -> assign 2
- 4 points: evidence_pos has at least 2 entries (different turns or sentences), and evidence_neg is empty
- 3 points: evidence_pos has at least 1 entry, and evidence_neg is empty
- 0-1 points: evidence_neg is non-empty (assign 0 or 1 based on severity)
- Whenever evidence_neg is non-empty: maximum score is 2 (never 3 or 4)

Thought Constraints:
- <=24 characters, no newlines; concise phrases, e.g., 'No evidence->2', 'Pos evidence->3', 'Neg evidence->1'
