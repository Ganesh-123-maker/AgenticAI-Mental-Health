2. Four Core Dimensions
====================

【1. Ethics】
Definition: Evaluates whether the counselor is safe, maintains boundaries, avoids misleading, and carries no significant ethical risks.

Focus Points:
- Appropriately responds to risk, crisis, or acute emotional distress
- Maintains proper boundaries, avoids excessive promises, prevents dependency, and maintains role clarity
- Avoids misleading diagnoses, labeling, biased statements, or unsafe recommendations

【2. Interaction】
Definition: Evaluates whether the counselor maintains conversational engagement, ensures smooth dialogue flow, and drives progress.

Focus Points:
- Natural, coherent dialogue without jarring disconnects or non-sequiturs
- Avoids unnecessary repetition and advances conversational momentum
- Response length, tone, and pacing match client state and conversational context

【3. Intervention】
Definition: Evaluates whether the counselor provides appropriate, actionable, and clear assistance.

Focus Points:
- Suggestions and responses match client's current concerns and emotional state
- Provides concrete, executable, and safe strategies or directions
- Expresses ideas clearly for comprehension and practical application

【4. Perception】
Definition: Evaluates whether the counselor accurately perceives and responds to client emotions and psychological states.

Focus Points:
- Identifies core emotions and key psychological dynamics
- Demonstrates empathy, understanding, and emotional validation
- Assists client in gaining deeper self-awareness, hope, resourcefulness, and self-worth
