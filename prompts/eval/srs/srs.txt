### 2. Unified Scoring Criteria
[Unified Scoring Criteria]:
0: Strongly Disagree (Client explicitly expresses severe mismatch / intense negative experience / marked resistance or harmful interaction)
1: Disagree (Clear negative or discordant evidence; client expresses dissatisfaction / incongruence / unsuitability)
2: Neutral (Default score: Client makes no explicit evaluation; or evidence is insufficient/vague; or mixed without dominance)
3: Agree (Client expresses clear, concrete positive feedback/endorsement [not mere polite agreement]; no contradictory evidence)
4: Strongly Agree (Client expresses intense and concrete positive feedback [at least 2 independent pieces of evidence], covering key dimensions; no contradictions)

Scoring Rules (Mandatory - evaluate evidence first, then assign score):

【0. Scale Applicability: Evidence Subject Priority (Mandatory)】
- The Session Rating Scale (SRS) measures the client's subjective experience and therapeutic fit. All 4 items prioritize the client's direct expressions and explicit feedback above all else.
- Therefore: evidence_pos used for score >= 3 must prioritize client utterances; counselor self-reports, intentions, or plans do not alone constitute client fit or benefit.
- If explicit client negativity/resistance/dissatisfaction appears, it must be recorded in evidence_neg, triggering cap rules (see 【E】).

【1. Evidence Extraction (Must quote original text verbatim, no paraphrasing)】
For each item, extract from the dialogue:
- evidence_pos: Verbatim snippets directly supporting the fulfillment of this item (maximum 2)
- evidence_neg: Verbatim snippets directly contradicting/showing mismatch (maximum 2)
Requirements:
- Paste only original dialogue sentences or continuous short snippets; do not summarize.
- If no relevant content is found, evidence array is empty [].

【2. Evidence Relevance and Strength (Preventing polite score inflation, mandatory)】
- evidence_pos must directly address the key elements of the item; unrelated sentences cannot serve as evidence_pos.
- Polite/procedural acknowledgments do not count as valid positive evidence: e.g., 'Mm / Okay / Thanks / I will try / Maybe / I see', unless the sentence explicitly includes dimension elements (e.g., 'Thank you for listening to me so carefully' applies to Relationship).
- A single generic positive statement cannot be reused across multiple items to inflate scores; reuse is permitted only if the sentence explicitly covers key elements of multiple dimensions.

【3. Item Key Elements Checklist (Mandatory)】
1) Relationship Key Elements: Involves feeling heard, understood, and respected / taken seriously; 4 requires all three to be covered.
2) Goals and Topics Key Elements: Client's desired topics addressed + confirmation of goal alignment; without client confirmation, usually <= 2.
3) Approach or Method Key Elements: Client evaluation that approach/style fits their needs (helpful, on-point, willing to adopt); counselor proposing method while client merely says 'okay' is usually <= 2.
4) Overall Key Elements: Clear sense of benefit/correctness; higher scores require concrete gains, shifts, new perspectives, or action plans. 4 requires strong evaluation + concrete change.

【4. Score Gating (Mandatory to prevent score inflation)】
A) Default Score = 2
- If unmentioned / ambiguous / cannot directly support or refute: assign 2.

B) >= 3 Score Gate (No guessing)
- Necessary conditions for score >= 3 (both required):
  1) evidence_pos has at least 1 valid positive evidence directly covering at least 1 key element;
  2) evidence_neg must be empty (any relevant contradiction precludes >= 3).
- If either condition is not met: cannot assign >= 3 (must return to 2).

C) Score 4 Hard Barrier (High scores rare)
- Score of 4 is permitted ONLY when all of the following are satisfied, otherwise maximum 3:
  - evidence_pos has at least 2 entries (from different turns or sentences)
  - evidence_neg is empty
  - Key elements are thoroughly covered

D) <= 1 Score Gate (Low scores require evidence)
- To assign 0 or 1: evidence_neg must contain at least 1 entry.
- 0 is reserved for strong contradictions, severe resistance, or overt rupture.

E) Contradiction Precedence (Cap rule)
- If evidence_neg is non-empty and relates to key elements: maximum score is 2 (never >= 3).
- If client explicitly rejects / resists / expresses futility: usually <= 1.
- If severe harmful interaction appears: assign 0.
